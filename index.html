Study tips that really work

Small habits that help you learn faster and remember longer.

⏱ 25 + 5 rule

Study for 25 minutes, rest for 5. After four rounds, take a longer break.

🧠 Recall, don't re-read

Close the book and write what you remember. Then check what you missed.

🔁 Revise on a schedule

Look at a topic again after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days. The Practice tab does this for you.

😴 Sleep before exams

Sleep helps your brain store what you learned. Avoid all-night study.

Questions

Can I use it without internet?

Yes, after you save lessons in the Library. Offline you can read lessons, listen to them, take quizzes, use the formula sheet and periodic table, and get answers to many basic questions. New AI answers need internet.

Is the AI always right?

No. AI can make mistakes. Use StudentAI to understand steps, and always check important answers with your teacher or textbook.

Which languages can I use?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Bengali and Marathi. Voice input and read-aloud depend on the voices installed on your device.

Can I send a photo of my homework?

Yes. Press the 📷 button. Turn on "Check my work" to have the tutor find the first mistake in your working.
